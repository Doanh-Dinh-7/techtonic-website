# TechTonic Website V2.0 Overview

## Muc Tieu

Nang cap TechTonic Website thanh **Modern Futuristic 3D Experience**: premium dark mode, glassmorphism, neon accents, subtle 3D, motion muot va noi dung bam sat tai lieu chinh thuc `Noi dung website.docx`.

Website V2.0 can giu ban sac CLB sinh vien IT Da Nang: hoc thuat nghiem tuc, cong dong gan ket, vui ve, tre trung va co tinh cong nghe cao.

## Nguyen Tac San Pham

- Noi dung chinh thuc nam trong `lib/content`, component chi phu trach hien thi.
- 3D la lop tang trai nghiem, khong duoc lam hong kha nang doc noi dung chinh.
- Dark mode la mac dinh: nen slate/black, glass surface, neon cyan/purple/magenta/electric blue.
- Responsive mobile-first, hover tren desktop phai co fallback tap/focus tren mobile.
- Uu tien performance: lazy-load 3D, WebGL fallback, cap DPR, ton trong `prefers-reduced-motion`.
